An interactive world map of the books you've read, coloured by where each author comes from.

Import your library from Goodreads or StoryGraph and BookMap looks up every author on Wikidata to place them on the map, with the option to correct any it gets wrong.

Click a country you haven't read from yet to see its best-known books and find your next read.
